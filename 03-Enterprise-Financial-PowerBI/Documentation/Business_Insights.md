## Evidence-based findings

### 1. North was the highest-profit region

North recorded simplified operating profit of ₹21,425,334.79, approximately 27.06% of total simplified operating profit. Compare its revenue, expenses, and operating margin with the other regions to investigate what may explain this result.

### 2. Operation was the largest expense category

Operation expenses were ₹56,754,377.39, approximately 20.43% of total expenses. Break these costs down by month, region, and department to find major drivers and opportunities for responsible cost optimization.

### 3. February 2024 had the largest monthly revenue shortfall

The revenue variance in February 2024 was −₹527,111.30, meaning actual revenue was below the month's budget by this amount. Explore region, department, and product performance during that month to identify the largest contributors.

## Recommended actions

1. Benchmark regional revenue, expense, and margin performance; investigate whether practices linked with North's result can be adapted elsewhere.
2. Review Operation expenses by month, region, and department; validate proposed savings against operational needs.
3. Investigate the February 2024 shortfall by region, department, and product; use the result to improve future forecasting and monitoring.
4. Establish a regular review of actual-versus-budget results and investigate large deviations promptly.

## Interpretation limitations

- These results show what occurred in the dataset; they do not establish why it occurred.
- The simplified operating profit measure is revenue minus expenses and may differ from formal accounting definitions.
- The budget table contains monthly budgets by region and department but has no product or expense-category allocation. Avoid claiming valid product-level revenue variance or category-level expense variance.
- Spending below budget is a favorable budget variance, but it does not on its own establish that spending was efficient or sufficient.
